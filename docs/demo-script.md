browser tabs out of the recording. This is a script, not a published demo video.

"NEAR Pulse asks whether real usage tells us more about ecosystem adoption than
token price. These are verified historical observations, not a live feed. The
research verdict is deliberately insufficient data."

**12-30s - Network Activity.**
"Here are November 3 through 9 transaction counts and distinct signing accounts
from NEAR's public BigQuery dataset. Accounts are not people. Contract calls
are verified only on November 9; the other dates stay missing. We can inspect
exact daily rows, download CSV and trace the source SQL."

**30-47s - Intents Economy.**
"The same seven days contain 134,156 successful 1Click swaps and approximately
263.03 million dollars of input volume. The CSV retains exact decimal totals.
This is 1Click activity, not all Intents activity; days follow swap creation."

**47-67s - Thesis Lab.**
"The indexed chart compares available adoption signals. Seven days yield six
paired daily changes, below our 30-pair threshold. A private price API check
worked, but publication rights are unconfirmed, so prices remain unavailable.
We cannot claim that usage outperforms price or causes token demand."

**67-85s - Methodology.**
"Every observation traces to source exports, hashes and query jobs. Offline
replay rebuilds the dashboard without credentials or new paid queries. The
thesis includes contrary explanations and falsification rules. NEAR Pulse is
an independent Investment Thesis submission, separate from Sentinel."
